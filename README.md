# Donka-Phi-Pisa

The game is a 2-4 player co-op horror-adventure game for PC centered around a group of pledge brothers rushing a fictional fraternity at a fictional university. As part of their pledging process, the players are dropped off in a remote forest and tasked with finding their way back to civilization. What begins as a seemingly harmless fraternity challenge quickly takes a dark turn when the group discovers that the forest is haunted. Players must work together, survive supernatural threats, and find a way to escape.
